Help & how-to

Everything Converse does, and how to use it. Click a section to expand.

🚀 Getting started

Demo (no setup): on Home, click Demo · strong call to watch the areas fill green, or Demo · rough call to see the copilot nudge you. Demos replay a scripted call in under a minute — no keys, no mic.

Go live: add a Deepgram API key in Settings (and an Anthropic or OpenAI key for sharper questions), then click Go live. Allow your microphone (that's you), and share your meeting tab with audio (that's the prospect). Your two voices are transcribed on separate channels, so a rep asking about budget never marks budget covered.

🟢 The live overlay
  • The 8 dots (left): your qualification areas. grey = not covered, amber = partial, green = covered. The most overdue area pulses. Click any dot to see its evidence, confidence, and an “Ask about this now” button.
  • Score (e.g. 3/8) and talk meter (you vs prospect) update live in the top bar.
  • Coaching stats (top-left): questions asked and your speaking pace (wpm).
  • Suggestion card (bottom): a single bridging question at a natural pause — or instantly when you hit “Ask now”. Auto-dismisses after 25s.
  • Objection card (amber, bottom-right): pops when the prospect objects, with the matching battlecard.
  • Transcript / Notes buttons open side panels. Notes are saved into the summary.

Hotkeys: Esc close/dismiss/end · S snooze the nudged area 5 min · T transcript · N notes.

🎯 Live coaching

Coaching watches how you run the call and nudges you in real time:

  • Monologue — you've been talking too long without a pause; ask a question.
  • Talk ratio — you're talking too much of the time; let them talk.
  • Too few questions — get them talking with an open question.

The summary shows your talk ratio, questions asked, pace, and longest monologue (amber = worth improving).

⚠️ Objection detection & battlecards

When the prospect raises a price, timing, competitor, authority, skepticism, or status-quo objection, an amber card surfaces the most relevant battlecard from your Context docs so you have an answer in the moment.

To make these useful, add battlecards in the Context tab (e.g. “battlecard-vs-workday”). Objections raised are listed in the summary.

🔀 Frameworks (MEDDPICC / BANT / SPICED)

By default the copilot tracks MEDDPICC (8 areas). In Settings → Framework switch live calls to BANT (Budget, Authority, Need, Timeline) or SPICED — the rail, time budgets, and classifier all adapt. (Demos always use MEDDPICC.)

📄 Context docs

Drop your battlecards, product one-pagers, ICP notes, and pricing into the Context tab (Markdown, one topic per file). The copilot pulls the most relevant doc into each suggested question and matches battlecards to objections — so guidance is grounded in your product, not generic.

🎛️ Persona, models & custom prompts
  • AI provider — use Anthropic (Claude) or OpenAI (GPT). Paste the matching API key and pick the provider; the model names auto-adjust if you don't set your own.
  • Persona — set who you're selling to (e.g. “CFO — ROI & risk”) so questions match what they care about.
  • Models & cooldown — choose the model and how often nudges may fire.
  • Custom prompts — override the classifier and question prompts entirely (blank = built-in; the default is shown as a placeholder to copy).
📊 Summary, exports & dashboard

After a call you get a summary: coverage grid with the exact prospect quote behind each area, talk ratio, coaching stats, objections, notes, and what's still open. Export it with:

  • Copy / Copy CRM fields — to clipboard.
  • Download / Transcript — Markdown files.
  • Email — a pre-filled draft · Slack — posts to your webhook (set in Settings) · Drive — uploads to Google Drive.

The Dashboard aggregates all calls: average coverage, per-area cover rate, a coverage trend, and overall talk ratio.

🗑️ Your data (save, backup, delete)

Every call is saved locally (nothing leaves your machine except what you export to your own keys/Drive). In History you can delete one call, Export backup (.json), Export & wipe all, or Delete all. Wiping can't be undone — keep a backup if you might want the calls later.

💾 Google Drive & Slack setup

Drive (sign in from the app): in Google Cloud create an OAuth client (Web application or Desktop app) and add http://localhost:5173/oauth2callback as an authorized redirect URI. Point GOOGLE_OAUTH_CLIENT in .env at the downloaded JSON, then click Connect Google Drive in Settings — you'll sign in with Google in your browser, no terminal needed. (You can still use npm run connect-drive if you prefer.)

Google requires that one-time OAuth client — there's no way around it — but after that it's a single click.

Slack: create an incoming webhook and paste its URL into Settings → Slack webhook URL. Then use Slack on the summary.
